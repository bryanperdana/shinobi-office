# Changelog

## 0.8.0 — first public cut

- **Hermes Desktop plugin:** route `/office` with real-signal statuses, per-session owner attribution,
  Mission Board from Kanban, approval gong, genin for subagents, and an activity feed.
- **Two-storey HQ:** stair navigation, floor tabs, and a see-through slab when viewed from above.
- **Village:** dojo, library, zen garden, tea house, and Japanese countryside (paddies, torii, river, minka,
  mountains) with day/night.
- **Idle life:** roaming, sparring, a curated chat bank and ninja-cat archetype, with a configurable owner
  name.
- **Camera:** 360° orbit, wall cutaway, and over-the-shoulder laptop screens showing status only.
- **Performance:** static baking, instancing, an fps cap and Auto/High/Eco quality.
- **Config:** `config.js` / `config.local.js` presets and deterministic archetypes for new profiles.
- **Web demo:** a static, self-hostable page.
